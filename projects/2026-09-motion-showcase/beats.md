# Motion showcase: what lands on each beat

The cut as built, beat by beat. Frames are video frames at 30 fps (seconds = frame / 30); beat n hits on frame
26 + 15n, two frames ahead of the music. `storyboard.md` is the plan and its colour script; this is what's on screen.

## Pickup and bar 1 · 0–85 · 01 — SQUASH & STRETCH

The product's name, THE NEW BUY BOX., a word a beat, each as big as the frame allows: Archivo 900 at a 560 px cap
(half the frame's height), its width solved to span 91% of the frame, standing on the ground line.

- **0**: THE fills the frame, whole, its far letters still settling from a stamp just before the video starts; the red
  drop (340 px) rises out of a landing at the left. The HUD boots: brackets grow from the corners, every label decodes.
- **11** (pickup hit): the drop lands on THE's T at 3.4:1 squash with a diamond, sparks, a ring and a dent that rings;
  T and H dip into the line.
- **26 · 41 · 56**: a landing a beat, a step to the right each time, and each one's shock runs out along the line,
  punching the old word's letters down through it as the next word's stamp down from above: NEW, then BUY, then BOX,
  each settled by the second frame after its hit. Every "and" is an apex.
- **71**: the last landing, on a pad after BOX: the drop is its full stop, and crouches flat for five frames.
- **76–85**: it launches, and swells to the frame's centre until red covers every pixel on 85.

## Bar 4 · 206–265 · 04 — INK × 174

  wave inks the dots around it in a burst about 790 px across, and the camera punches 5% about the strike. The count

- **207–216**: the strike ripples outward, each ring of dots flashing in its own ink, red-orange to amber, and the

- **236**: strike 3, right: violet to magenta; INKS. slams in beside the count from 234, whole on 236, and the count

  count and INKS. push in 2% over 262–265 into bar 5's punch.
