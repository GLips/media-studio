# Motion showcase: what lands on each beat

The cut as built, beat by beat. Frames are video frames at 30 fps (seconds = frame / 30). Each bar's beats come from
the bar table in `timeline.ts` (4, 4, 6, 6, 4, 4, 4, 8 and 4 beats), every hit two frames ahead of the music;
`node tools/bar-clock.ts` prints each beat's frame. `storyboard.md` is the plan and its colour script; this is what's
on screen.

## Pickup and bar 1 · 0–85 · 01 — SQUASH & STRETCH

The product's name, THE NEW BUY BOX., a word a beat, each as big as the frame allows: Archivo 900 at a 560 px cap
(half the frame's height), its width solved so its ink spans 88% of the frame, standing on the ground line.

- **0**: THE fills the frame, whole, its far letters still settling from a stamp just before the video starts; the red
  drop (340 px) rises out of a landing at the left. The HUD boots: brackets grow from the corners, every label decodes.
- **10** (pickup hit): the drop lands on THE's T at 3.4:1 squash with a diamond; on 11 come sparks, a ring and a dent
  that rings, and T and H dip into the line.
- **25 · 40 · 55**: a landing a beat, a step to the right each time, and each one's shock runs out along the line,
  punching the old word's letters down through it as the next word's stamp down from above: NEW, then BUY, then BOX,
  each settled by the second frame after its hit. Every "and" is an apex.
- **70**: the last landing, on a pad after BOX: the drop is its full stop, and crouches flatter for six frames, 691 px
  across by 76.
- **77–85**: it launches, and swells to the frame's centre until red covers every pixel on 85.
