# Ingest

A recording has landed, or the person asks what's worth posting. Find the posts in it and propose them; make none.

## 1. Log it

The recordings: `studio library recordings --inbox` lists takes not yet added (`studio library add <folder>`);
`studio library list` the rest. For each new one, read its transcript whole (`studio library show <item>`, every
spoken track interleaved) and skim its picture (`studio library look <item> screen --every 15 --columns 2`). Then
read the recent recordings it may answer or be answered by: the same feature, the same agent, the same problem.

Done when every minute of the new recordings is accounted for, and each earlier one it could call back to is named.

## 2. Propose

Find each moment the craft would post: a story with its payoff, its cold open if it has one, and the stretches whose
picture shows it. Most work sessions hold zero or one; say so when there's none rather than stretch one.

For each proposal, give in the chat:

- **The story in one sentence**, and the moment that opens it, word for word with its item and time.
- **Its stretches**, by item and range, in the order it plays, with the running length.
- **Its verdict**: usable as recorded, or what it needs first (a pickup line, a clean recapture of a screen, a
  private range cleared), each with why.

Done when the person has picked one to `cut`, or passed on them all.
